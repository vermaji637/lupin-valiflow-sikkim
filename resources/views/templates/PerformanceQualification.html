1.0 OBJECTIVE

To establish documentary evidence to demonstrate that machine ID No. located at in the facility of Lupin is qualified for performance as per this protocol.

2.0 SCOPE

The scope of this document is applicable for:

The performance qualification of machine I.D No. under the following condition/s:

  • New / first installation
  • Major modification after installation
  • Shifted from one location to another

This protocol evaluates performance considering worst case scenarios and key parameters.

Challenges of Key points / parameters

Challenges of worst case scenario for each equipment.

Reporting and handling the deviations observed (if any).

3.0 RESPONSIBILITY

Responsibilities of different department/ personnel involved in different activities related to the performance qualification of machine I.D No. is defined below:

Validation Department

  • Preparation of PQ Protocol in consultation with Engineering / Projects if required.
  • Checking the protocol for accuracy and adequacy prior to the approval.
  • Preparation of PQ Report.

Engineering / Production Department

  • Providing required information to Validation department for preparation of the protocol.
  • Execution of protocol in-coordination with user department.
  • Checking the protocol and report for adequacy and accuracy.

Quality Assurance Department

  • Providing guidance, wherever required to the related departments.
  • Review and approval of the protocol.
  • Review and approval of the performance qualification report.

7.0 SUMMARY AND CONCLUSION

On completion of PQ activity, the data generated shall be collected, compiled, critically reviewed, evaluated; any deviations observed shall be discussed and resolved. Based on the final results and discussions a brief summary (PQ Report) shall be prepared and shall contain the following:

  • Objective
  • Methodology
  • Observations / results and evaluation
  • Deviation and corrective actions taken
  • Conclusion

The discussion of PQ results (PQ Report) shall be enclosed as Attachment-1.
