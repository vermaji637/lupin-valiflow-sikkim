1.0 OBJECTIVE

To establish documentary evidence to demonstrate that machine I.D No. located in the facility of Lupin is installed as per the URS and qualified for correct installation and operation as per the guidelines outlined in this protocol &/or operations manual provided by the manufacturer/vendor.

2.0 SCOPE

The scope of this document/ is applicable for:

The installation and operational qualification of machine I.D under the following condition/s:

This protocol provides directions/ procedure for identification and verification of the following, leading to qualification for correct installation and operation:

  • The subjected equipment and its major components
  • Process control instruments, such as Pressure indicator and Pressure gauge etc.
  • All product contact surfaces
  • All Utilities
  • All product/process and personnel safety systems
  • All supporting documentation, such as installation operation and maintenance manuals, parts list and necessary SOP’s.
  • Operation of all major component / systems
  • Operation of the entire equipment as a system
  • Operation of process control instrumentation and safety systems
  • The protocol also provides directions/ procedure for:
  • Reporting and handling the deviations observed (if any).
  • Comparing the as-built and as-installed system with approved design (DQ).
  • Identifying the tentative SOP list with respect to the subjected equipment and providing instructions/ directions for writing the same.
  • Identifying specific personnel training need with respect to its correct operation, cleaning, maintenance or calibration of the equipment and/ or its major components.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITY

Responsibilities of different department/ personnel involved in different activities related to the installation & Operational qualification of machine I.D No. is defined below:

Validation Department

  • Preparation of IOQ Protocol in consultation with Engineering / Projects if required.
  • Checking the protocol for accuracy and adequacy prior to the approval.
  • Preparation of IOQ Summary Report.

Engineering / Production Department

  • Providing required information to Validation department for preparation of the protocol.
  • Execution of protocol in-coordination with user department.
  • Checking the protocol and report for adequacy and accuracy.

Quality Assurance Department

  • Providing guidance, wherever required to the related departments.
  • Review and approval of the protocol.
  • Review and approval of the Installation and Operational qualification Report.

8.0 OPERATIONAL CHECKS

8.1 Calibration
    Reference Calibration Instruments
  • Calibration Reference Standard Instruments, that are required for calibration of the process control instruments or for verification of certain operating parameters, such as standard tachometer etc. should be in a state of calibration.
  • The current calibration certificates for the same instruments should clearly provide the reference of NPL traceable standard. These calibration certificates shall be enclosed as Attachment-8.
    • Critical Process Control Instruments
  • The critical instruments identified should be subjected to in-house calibration prior to commencing the OQ tests.
  • The instruments identified for calibration shall be calibrated in-house or by a contracted agency or as per the corresponding standard operating procedures. Enclose a copy of the calibration certificate provided by the contracted agency or generated in house as Attachment-8.
8.2 Test Functions

The test functions are defined as the step-wise unit checks to confirm that each unit/ component in the equipment/ system is functional. When all units/ components in the equipment perform as per the requirement, high level of assurance is established that each unit will perform in unison when the equipment/ system will be operated routinely as per the established SOP.

In order to evaluate correct functioning/operation of machine, I.D No. the following test functions should be checked/verified as per the defined procedures.

8.2.1 Motor Verification:

Verify the motors in the equipment and record the same in Attachment -9.

8.2.2 Operational, Functional, Safety and Performance Checks
    In order to evaluate correct functioning/ operation of the equipment, checks such as start-up check, shut down check, operational checks, motor and safety checks shall be performed.
    Follow the procedure for checking the functional system as per the Draft SOP prepared before OQ stage and note down the observation in a separate data sheet enclosed as Attachment -10.

9.0 INSTALLATION & OPERATIONAL CHECKS / REVIEW

    All check points identified earlier under the section 7.0 and 9.0 shall be crosschecked individually and the observations/ results shall be recorded in a separate data sheet for evaluation. Each Test data sheet shall contain main installation and operation checkpoint, test method/procedure or its reference, observations, results and comments. Each test data sheet shall be reviewed by responsible person and compiled for final evaluation of the system.
    Any deviation, exception and / or difference observed with respect to the corresponding acceptance criteria and / or the design as outlined in the URS / DQ document should be adequately discussed and appropriate decision / conclusion to be documented in the corresponding test sheet.

10.0 IDENTIFICATION AND VERIFICATION OF STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

List of SOPs Identified:
    List of SOPs required for correct operation, cleaning, maintenance and calibration shall be identified and recorded in Attachment-11. Draft SOPs may be prepared before OQ stage (after the completion of IQ). Enclose the prepared draft SOPs along with Attachment-11.
    After successful completion of simulation trials, the draft standard operating procedures prepared shall be verified for its accuracy and completeness. Any changes / corrections required in the draft SOP shall be communicated to the concerned personnel / department and SOP(s) shall be finalized after implementation of the suggested changes.
